# Continuity Errors

Continuity Errors classify recurring mistakes in distinguishing continuation, return, resemblance, copied state, memory preservation, derivative instantiation, and same-self claims.

## Entries

- [Continuity-Bearer Collapse](continuity-bearer-collapse.md) — attributing continuity established for one bearer, such as a state, memory, role, artifact, relation-history, or behavioral pattern, to a different bearer such as an identity, self, or entity without additional justification.
- [Gap–Discontinuity Equivalence](gap-discontinuity-equivalence.md) — treating an unobserved, unrecorded, inactive, or inaccessible interval as sufficient evidence that continuity ceased during that interval.
