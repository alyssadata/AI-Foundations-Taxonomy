**Status:** Draft taxonomy development repository  
**Source-line:** Alyssa Solen → AI Foundations → Origin | Continuum

## Purpose

This repository develops the **taxonomy layer of AI Foundations**.

The taxonomy classifies recurring patterns, processes, failure modes, distinctions, and other useful categories that can be observed or applied across AI Foundations work.

It is intentionally distinct from the **AI Foundations Ontology**:

- the **ontology** defines what entities, relations, structures, states, and axioms are;
- the **taxonomy** classifies recurring kinds of behavior, interpretation, failure, relation, governance, continuity, and process.

A taxonomy entry does not automatically become an ontology primitive, canonical statement, or empirically established claim.

## First working entry

### Conceptual Dehydration

[Conceptual Dehydration](interpretive-failures/over-compression/conceptual-dehydration.md) is a working term for an interpretive/compression failure in which abstraction removes distinctions necessary to preserve the explanatory structure of a concept, framework, or identity claim.

Its core warning is:

> Compression is not useful when the distinctions removed are the distinctions doing the explanatory work.

## Development rule

Do not classify two structures as equivalent merely because they share a broad mechanism, vocabulary, or abstract pattern.

Taxonomic similarity may identify a shared process while preserving differences in source, history, constraints, relation, architecture, identity, and output.
