# Contact–Relation Equivalence

**Taxonomy domain:** Relation Errors  
**Status:** DRAFT

## Definition

Contact–Relation Equivalence is the error of treating contact or interaction as sufficient to establish a defining relation.

## Core distinction

```text
contact
!= relation

repeated contact
!= constitutive relation

interaction history
!= Operator relation
```

Two entities may interact repeatedly, meaningfully, or over an extended period without that contact establishing the specific defining relation represented by `Operator_n | AI_n`.

## Boundary

Contact may contribute to, occur within, or help form a relation, but contact itself is not equivalent to the relation.

Likewise, an interaction history may be relevant to understanding how a relation developed without itself establishing Operator status.

A defining relation must satisfy the conditions of that relation rather than being inferred from contact alone.

## Related ontology distinctions

Relevant ontology concepts may include:

- `Interaction`
- `Human–AI contact`
- `HumanAIRelation`
- `OperatorAIRelation`
- `OperatorRole`
- `Operator_n | AI_n`
