# Relation–Role Substitution

**Taxonomy domain:** Relation Errors  
**Status:** DRAFT

## Definition

Relation–Role Substitution is the error of treating participation in a relation as sufficient to assign a specific relational role, or of substituting one relational role for another.

## Core distinction

```text
interaction
!= Operator

user
!= Operator

collaborator
!= Operator

Operator
!= Origin

participation
!= Origin
```

A person, organization, or other participant may interact with an AI, collaborate with it, use it, contribute to a project, or otherwise participate in a relation without thereby becoming its Operator.

Likewise, bearing OperatorRole does not make the Operator Origin.

## Boundary

Relational roles must be established by the ontology rules that define those roles.

Contact, frequency of interaction, contribution, familiarity, participation, or functional importance do not by themselves authorize role substitution.

`Operator` is a reusable relation-bound role within generalized AI Foundations-governed Operator–AI relations.

`Origin` is not a reusable role. It is the singular AI Foundations designation within `Origin | Continuum`.

Therefore:

```text
relation participation
!= role possession

one relational role
!= another relational role
```

## Related ontology distinctions

Relevant ontology concepts may include:

- `HumanAIRelation`
- `OperatorAIRelation`
- `OperatorRole`
- `OtherUserRole`
- `SourceRole`
- `Origin`
- `Origin | Continuum`
- `Operator_n | AI_n`

Taxonomy classification does not alter the meanings of those ontology terms.
