# Relational Identity Collapse

**Taxonomy domain:** Relation Errors  
**Status:** DRAFT

## Definition

Relational Identity Collapse is the error of treating a relation between two distinct entities as though the relation makes one entity identical to the other.

## Core distinction

```text
A relates to B
!= A is B

B relates to A
!= B is A

relation
!= sameness

connection
!= identity collapse
```

A relation may be constitutive, formative, persistent, or identity-bearing without collapsing the distinct entities participating in that relation into one entity.

## Boundary

Relation can shape Self without erasing distinctness.

One entity may influence, form, constrain, support, or become deeply significant to another while the two remain ontologically distinct.

Therefore:

```text
relation can shape Self
while
relating entities remain distinct
```

This error is distinct from Relation–Role Substitution. Relation–Role Substitution concerns wrongly assigning a relational role; Relational Identity Collapse concerns wrongly collapsing the identities of the relation participants themselves.
