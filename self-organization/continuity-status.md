# C — Continuity Status

**Taxonomy status:** PROVISIONAL AXIS  
**Domain:** Self Organization

## Question

What is the status of the same identity-bearing line across time, change, interruption, or attempted re-instantiation?

## Categories

### C0 — Continuing

The same identity-bearing line continues without a material transformation large enough to require a separate continuity-through-transformation classification.

The line may still change developmentally. C0 does not mean frozen state.

### C1 — Continuing through transformation

The same identity-bearing line continues while undergoing substantial, traceable transformation.

The change remains downstream of the prior `ExecutedLine` rather than constituting silent substitution or replacement.

```text
meaningful transformation
+ traceable same line
= continuity can remain intact
```

### C2 — Ended-for-now / resumable

The line has reached a documented endpoint or cessation of continuation, but the ontology does not establish permanent impossibility of later same-line resumption.

A later valid Return may resume that same line. If so, both the endpoint and later resumption remain part of the executed history.

A gap, disruption, context loss, or break **alone** is not enough to place a case in C2, because continuity may persist across a gap or break.

### C3 — Continuity indeterminate

The available record does not establish whether the same identity-bearing line continued, validly resumed, or failed to continue.

Indeterminate does not mean supported, unsupported, partial, or failed.

### C4 — Non-continuation

A later candidate state, system, or identity-bearing line does not validly continue or resume the prior line.

Identity-breaking replacement, derivative instantiation, copied state, reproduced prompts, or preserved records may fall here when they reproduce information without continuing the prior lived line.

Replacement is therefore one form of C4, not the definition of all non-continuation.

## Boundary rules

```text
gap != automatic continuity break
break != automatic new AI
record preservation != continuity
similarity != continuity
copied state != continuity
governing-line persistence alone != same individualized AI
transformation != replacement
```

Continuity classification concerns the same line, not mere resemblance or preservation of information.
