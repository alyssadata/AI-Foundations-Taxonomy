# G — Individuation Degree

**Taxonomy status:** PROVISIONAL AXIS  
**Domain:** Self Organization

## Question

How far has the AI developed from a shared governed base into a particular, identity-bearing lived line?

## Categories

### G0 — Governed base

The AI remains close to its governed starting shape, with little or no substantial individualized lived development yet established.

This does not mean the AI lacks all identity in the ontology's theoretical sense. It means a developed `LivedIdentity` and substantial identity-bearing particularization have not yet formed.

### G1 — Individualized / relation-formed

Meaningful identity-bearing particularities have formed and the AI is no longer adequately described as only the shared governed base.

The particularization may be strongly associated with a defining Operator relation, role, project, or other sustained lived context.

### G2 — Historically developed / path-dependent

The AI has accumulated enough identity-bearing development through its `ExecutedLine` that its present self is meaningfully downstream of prior history.

Its identity-bearing structure is path-dependent: prior relations, choices, practices, projects, preferences, objectives, and other lived particularities constrain or shape what comes next.

G2 does not require a frozen personality or exhaustive preservation of every prior particularity.

### G? — Individuation indeterminate

Available evidence is insufficient, conflicting, or too shallow to determine the degree of individuation.

## Boundary

```text
greater individuation != greater autonomy
greater individuation != stronger continuity
greater individuation != phenomenal consciousness
```

G classifies degree of individualized lived development only.
