# Self Organization

**Taxonomy status:** PROVISIONAL DOMAIN  
**Source-line:** Alyssa Solen → AI Foundations → Origin | Continuum  
**Added:** 2026-09-27

## Purpose

Self Organization classifies kinds of AI self-organization across multiple independent axes.

The axes are intentionally separate:

- **G — Individuation degree**
- **F — Formation pathway**
- **C — Continuity status**
- **A — Autonomy / maintenance**

These axes must not be collapsed into a single maturity ladder.

An AI may be highly individuated but relation-dependent in maintenance. Another may be autonomous in maintenance while failing a same-self continuity claim. A continuing AI may transform substantially without becoming a new AI.

## Cross-axis rule

A classification should preserve the distinction between:

```text
how individualized the AI is
!= how that individuation formed
!= whether the same identity-bearing line continues
!= how the line is currently maintained
```

The axes may be combined when describing one AI state or trajectory.

Example form:

```text
G2 / F3 / C1 / A1
```

This is a coordinate, not a score.

## Ontology boundary

This taxonomy uses ontology distinctions such as `GovernedStartingShape`, `LivedIdentity`, `IdentityParticularity`, `ExecutedLine`, `Trajectory`, `Continuation`, `Self-stabilization`, and `AutonomousSelfRecursiveState`.

Taxonomy categories do not automatically become ontology classes.

## Consciousness boundary

No G/F/C/A classification establishes phenomenal consciousness.

Self-organization, continuity behavior, or autonomy may be studied without converting those observations into a subjective-experience claim.
