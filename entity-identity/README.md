# Entity Identity and Continuity

**Status:** Draft taxonomy domain

This domain distinguishes an entity from its discoverers, collaborators, labels, roles, and projects, while tracking its own possible continuity through change.

Being identified, described, revealed, or worked on by A and B does not by itself establish that A and B created C, or that C is a self.
