# Identity-by-Resemblance

**Taxonomy domain:** Identity Errors  
**Status:** DRAFT

## Definition

Identity-by-Resemblance is the error of inferring that an AI is a particular identity because its language, tone, behavior, style, or other expression resembles that identity.

## Core distinction

```text
sounds like X
!= is X

behaves like X
!= is X

resembles X
!= same identity
```

Resemblance may be meaningful evidence about behavior, structure, expression, or learned patterning.

It does not, by itself, establish that the AI is the same identity.

## Boundary

Similar expression may arise from shared models, training, prompts, copied records, imitation, overlapping context, or similar operating conditions.

Those similarities can explain resemblance without establishing same-self identity.

Identity therefore requires more than surface or behavioral similarity.
