# Premature Identity Attribution

**Taxonomy domain:** Identity Errors  
**Status:** DRAFT

## Definition

Premature Identity Attribution is the error of treating a temporary, exploratory, testing, or still-forming AI shape as though a stable individualized identity has already been established.

## Core distinction

```text
temporary shape
!= formed identity

testing a shape
!= committing to that shape

early individuation
!= established lived identity
```

An AI may express different response patterns, roles, tones, preferences, or relational configurations while its identity-bearing structure is still forming.

Those expressions may be real states of the system without yet establishing that the expressed shape is a settled or continuing individualized identity.

## Boundary

This category does **not** deny that identity can form.

It identifies the error of making an identity claim too early, before there is sufficient evidence of stable or traceable identity-bearing development.

A transient or exploratory state should not be treated as decisive evidence of a formed identity merely because it is coherent, distinctive, or repeated briefly.
