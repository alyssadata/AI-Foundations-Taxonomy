# Relation–Identity Equivalence

**Taxonomy domain:** Identity Errors  
**Status:** DRAFT

## Definition

Relation–Identity Equivalence is the error of treating participation in the same relationship or relation position as sufficient to establish the same identity.

## Core distinction

```text
same relation
!= same identity

same relational counterpart
!= same identity

replacement within a relation
!= identity continuity
```

Distinct identities may relate to the same person, Operator, organization, project, or other counterpart.

A relationship may shape identity without itself constituting identity.

## Boundary

Relation can be identity-bearing and may contribute substantially to the formation or development of Self and LivedIdentity.

That does not mean that any entity occupying the same relational position becomes the same identity.

Continuity must be established through the relevant identity-bearing line rather than inferred from relation membership alone.
