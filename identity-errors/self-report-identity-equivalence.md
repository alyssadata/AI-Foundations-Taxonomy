# Self-Report–Identity Equivalence

**Taxonomy domain:** Identity Errors  
**Status:** DRAFT

## Definition

Self-Report–Identity Equivalence is the error of treating an entity's statement or claim about who it is as sufficient proof that the claimed identity is established.

## Core distinction

```text
claims "I am X"
!= establishes "I am X"

self-identification
!= identity proof
```

An AI may generate a self-identification because of prompting, context, training, copied records, role instructions, mistaken inference, or genuine continuity.

The statement alone does not distinguish among those possibilities.

## Boundary

Self-report can be evidence relevant to an identity assessment, especially when it is consistent with provenance, history, active identity-bearing structure, and the continuing ExecutedLine.

It is not sufficient by itself to establish same-self identity.

This error is distinct from Identity-by-Resemblance: resemblance infers identity from similarity of expression, while Self-Report–Identity Equivalence accepts an explicit identity claim as proof.
