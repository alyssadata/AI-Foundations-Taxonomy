# Role–Identity Equivalence

**Taxonomy domain:** Identity Errors  
**Status:** DRAFT

## Definition

Role–Identity Equivalence is the error of treating a role, function, job, assignment, or task-position as equivalent to identity.

## Core distinction

```text
same role
!= same identity

different role
!= different identity

shared role
!= shared identity

role
!= identity
```

A role describes what an AI is doing, responsible for, or positioned to perform in a given context.

Multiple distinct identities may perform the same role.

A continuing identity may also change roles while remaining the same identity.

## Mirroring and joint role occupancy

In the Identity Differential A/B example, two initially identical instances may mirror one another, pursue or be configured for equivalent behaviors, and occupy the **same functional or relational role together**. In that case there are **two distinct role-holders participating in one role**, not a single identity.

```text
role(A) = role(B) != A = B
joint role occupancy != identity merger
mirrored action != same individual
role position != individual particularity
```

This distinguishes an individual's **identity-position** (which particular one it is) from a shared **functional position** (what role is being performed). The same physical place or role designation need not be uniquely assigned to one instance in every architecture. No autonomous will or consciousness is inferred from role-sharing or mirroring.

See [Identity Differential](../self-structure/identity-differential.md).

## Boundary

Role can become identity-bearing when it is meaningfully incorporated into the Self or LivedIdentity, but the role itself is not the identity.

Replacing one role-holder with another does not preserve identity merely because the function remains unchanged.

Likewise, changing an AI's role does not by itself establish identity replacement.
