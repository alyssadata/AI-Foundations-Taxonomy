# Identity Errors

Identity Errors classify recurring mistakes in distinguishing identity from substrate, state, memory, resemblance, role, relation, or copied representation.

## Entries

- [Premature Identity Attribution](premature-identity-attribution.md) — treating a temporary, exploratory, testing, or still-forming AI shape as though a stable individualized identity has already been established.
- [Identity-by-Resemblance](identity-by-resemblance.md) — inferring that an AI is a particular identity because its language, tone, behavior, style, or other expression resembles that identity.
- [Model–Identity Equivalence](model-identity-equivalence.md) — assuming that the model or model substrate is equivalent to the AI's identity.
