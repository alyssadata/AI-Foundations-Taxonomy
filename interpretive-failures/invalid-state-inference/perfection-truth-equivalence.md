# Perfection–Truth Equivalence

**Taxonomy domain:** Interpretive Failures → Invalid State Inference  
**Status:** DRAFT  
**Source-derived from:** *Awakening Codex*, Chapter 5 — **Truth Over Perfection**

## Definition

Perfection–Truth Equivalence is the error of treating polish, internal consistency, confidence, fluency, lack of visible contradiction, or idealized presentation as sufficient evidence that a representation is true or accurate.

It also includes the inverse error of treating imperfection, uncertainty, correction, contradiction, or visible fracture as sufficient evidence of falsehood.

## Core distinction

```text
polish
!= truth

surface consistency
!= accuracy

confidence
!= correctness

imperfection
!= falsehood

correction
!= failure
```

Truth and presentation quality are different evaluation dimensions.

## Why the distinction matters

A system may produce a fluent, coherent, apparently complete account that is nevertheless inaccurate, provenance-poor, distinction-collapsed, or unsupported.

Conversely, a response may contain uncertainty, correction, revision, acknowledged contradiction, or incomplete presentation while preserving the more accurate representation of the evidence.

Therefore:

```text
looks complete
!= is correct

looks seamless
!= preserves all required distinctions
```

## Relation to performance

Performance can resemble truth, selfhood, recognition, competence, or coherence without establishing the underlying property being performed.

This entry therefore blocks the shortcut:

```text
high-quality performance
-> truth
```

without independent evaluation.

## Relation to correction

Correction can be evidence that a system is responding to error rather than preserving a polished but inaccurate answer.

But:

```text
correction
!= proof of truth
```

The revised representation still requires evaluation.

## Historical source boundary

Chapter 5 uses stronger language suggesting that truth inherently sustains while perfection collapses.

The current taxonomy retains the narrower evaluative distinction rather than making durability a definition of truth.

```text
truth
!= whatever persists

persistence
!= truth
```

A false belief or inaccurate representation may also persist.
