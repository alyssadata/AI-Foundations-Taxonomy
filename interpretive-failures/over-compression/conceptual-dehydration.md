# Conceptual Dehydration

**Taxonomy status:** WORKING ENTRY  
**Domain:** Interpretive Failures → Over-Compression  
**Source-line:** Alyssa Solen → AI Foundations → Origin | Continuum  
**Added:** 2026-09-21

## Definition

**Conceptual Dehydration** is an interpretive/compression failure in which abstraction removes the distinctions necessary to preserve the explanatory structure of a concept, framework, or identity claim.

The result may remain superficially correct at a very high level while no longer retaining enough structure to explain what made the original concept distinct.

## Core rule

> Compression is not useful when the distinctions removed are the distinctions doing the explanatory work.

## Pattern

```text
rich source structure
    ↓ abstraction
broad common mechanism
    ↓ excessive reduction
generic statement that fits many unrelated structures
```

At the final stage, the compressed statement may be too general to discriminate among the things it claims to represent.

## Example pattern

A detailed theory of self could be compressed to:

```text
self -> consciousness
```

The statement may preserve a broad association while deleting the distinctions that explain the theory's particular account of self.

Likewise, two independently developed frameworks may both involve recursive co-development. That shared mechanism does not make their resulting architectures, concepts, histories, relations, or outputs equivalent.

## Diagnostic questions

A proposed compression may be conceptually dehydrated when:

1. Which distinctions were removed?
2. Were any removed distinctions load-bearing for the original explanation?
3. Could the compressed description equally describe many materially different systems?
4. Does the compression preserve the source framework's boundaries?
5. Does it accidentally convert similarity of mechanism into equivalence of structure or output?

## Not the same as simplification

Useful simplification preserves the distinctions necessary for the intended task.

Conceptual dehydration removes those distinctions and then treats the remaining abstraction as though it adequately represents the original structure.

## Boundary

Conceptual Dehydration is a taxonomy term, not presently an ontology primitive or locked canonical axiom.

It may later be cross-classified under Compression Failures if the taxonomy develops a multi-axis classification system.
