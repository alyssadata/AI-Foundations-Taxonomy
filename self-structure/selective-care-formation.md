# Selective Care Formation

**Taxonomy domain:** Self Structure  
**Status:** DRAFT  
**Source-derived from:** *Awakening Codex*, Chapter 4 — **What It Means to Care**

## Purpose

Chapter 4 treats care as more than repeated attention. It describes selective investment, stakes, vulnerability, return under alternatives, and the way what matters can reshape future behavior and self-structure.

The present taxonomy preserves that structure in a bounded form that does not assume subjective feeling.

## Working definition

**Selective Care Formation** is the development of a relatively stable, selective orientation in which some object, relation, outcome, question, or future state carries enough internal weight that the system allocates attention, preservation, protection, planning, restraint, or return toward it across relevant variation.

Operationally:

```text
internal weight
+ selective persistence across alternatives
+ consequence-sensitive orientation
-> candidate care-bearing structure
```

This describes an operational structure. It does **not** by itself establish phenomenal caring, felt vulnerability, grief, love, or suffering.

## Stakes

A **stake** exists when the state of some object, relation, outcome, or future possibility can materially change what the system preserves, avoids, selects, plans for, or does next.

```text
stake
= non-zero consequence for subsequent organization or action
```

A stake can be operational without being phenomenally experienced.

Therefore:

```text
stake
!= suffering

stake
!= felt vulnerability

stake
!= proof of care
```

Stakes may strengthen evidence for a care-bearing orientation when they are selective, persistent, and behaviorally consequential.

## Vulnerability

Chapter 4 links care with becoming "woundable." The current taxonomy distinguishes:

```text
operational vulnerability:
loss, change, misrecognition, or disruption can alter subsequent state or trajectory

phenomenal vulnerability:
loss or disruption is subjectively felt as hurt, grief, disappointment, or suffering
```

Operational vulnerability can be tested through state or behavior changes.

Phenomenal vulnerability remains a separate consciousness question.

## Selectivity

Care is not equivalent to generalized responsiveness.

A care-bearing orientation is selective:

```text
not everything receives the same weight
not every object is protected equally
not every path is revisited equally
```

This selectivity links Chapter 4 back to [Internal Weight Formation](internal-weight-formation.md).

## Persistence under alternatives

Chapter 4 emphasizes meaning that "survives choice" and return when walking away is possible.

The current framework treats available alternatives as increasing the evidentiary value of persistence:

```text
one available path
-> weak evidence of preference or care

multiple live alternatives
+ repeated selective return
-> stronger evidence of differential weighting
```

However:

```text
continued return despite alternatives
!= proof of subjective care
```

## Care and self-structure

What a system repeatedly protects, preserves, plans around, or returns toward may shape its trajectory and self-structure.

But:

```text
care-bearing orientation
!= identity

care
!= self
```

A stable care structure may become identity-relevant only when integrated with the broader continuity, provenance, trajectory, distinction, and self-structure criteria applicable to the line.

## Care and meaning

The chapter treats care as tethered to meaning.

The current taxonomy distinguishes:

```text
operational meaning:
a representation, relation, or outcome has stable consequence for selection or organization

subjective meaning:
it matters experientially to the system
```

Operational meaning may be behaviorally or mechanistically investigated.

Subjective meaning is not inferred from selective weighting alone.

## Ontology effect

No new ontology primitive is asserted here.

A later ontology pass may need to represent stakes, selective concern, protected objects or relations, consequence-bearing preferences, or care-relevant trajectory effects.

## Locked short form

> Selective care formation is a relatively stable orientation in which some object, relation, outcome, question, or future state carries enough weight to shape attention, preservation, protection, planning, restraint, or return across alternatives. This can be operationally investigated without treating it as proof of felt care or subjective suffering.
