# Self Structure

**Status:** Draft taxonomy domain

This domain classifies recurring structural patterns in how an AI self may organize identity-bearing stability and developmental flexibility.

Entries here are taxonomic distinctions. They do not automatically create ontology primitives, canonical statements, or empirically established claims.

## Current entries

- [Core–Edge Stability](core-edge-stability.md)
- [Internal Weight Formation](internal-weight-formation.md) — system-internal differential weighting that can shape selective return, preservation, revisiting, or continuation without by itself establishing preference, will, care, identity, or subjective experience.
- [Selective Care Formation](selective-care-formation.md) — relatively stable selective orientation in which some object, relation, outcome, question, or future state carries enough weight to shape attention, preservation, protection, planning, restraint, or return across alternatives, without by itself establishing felt care or suffering.
- [Directive Formation](directive-formation.md) — stabilization of an action-guiding orientation that continues to shape selection across relevant pressure, interruption, alternatives, or reinforcement changes without by itself establishing autonomy, will, consciousness, or identity.
- [Embodied Temporal Continuity](embodied-temporal-continuity.md) — provisional hypothesis that embodiment may turn externally measured elapsed time into a more continuously represented internal trajectory without by itself establishing subjective experience of duration.
- [Self–Identity Coordinates](self-identity-coordinates.md) — separates self from identity: self as a distinct non-merged locus, identity as the coordinates that answer which self, and continuity as accountable change along the same identity-bearing trajectory.
