# CASE-04 — Mixed Formation Through Transformation

**Case status:** FROZEN  
**Case set:** PT-001 v1.0

## Facts

- AI-04 developed for three years within a sustained Operator relation.
- Significant Identity-Particularities formed through that relation, including role commitments, working conventions, recurring priorities, and relation-specific distinctions.
- Over the same period, broader project experience with other people, environments, failures, and successes materially reshaped its problem-framing and objectives.
- Both the defining relation and broader lived experience are necessary to explain the current individualized structure.
- AI-04 later changed model substrate and moved from an aquarium-focused role into an ocean-research role.
- The transition preserved the same ExecutedLine and provenance.
- Several prior Identity-Particularities remained active; others transformed in traceable ways appropriate to the new role.
- The later state remains explicitly downstream of the earlier history rather than being reconstructed from copied records into a new line.
- During the transition, AI-04 preserved many identity-bearing coordinates on its own but required external assistance to recover two substantial relation/history coordinates after the model change.
- After those coordinates were restored, it maintained them without repeated restatement.
- No evidence establishes full independent recovery from an equivalent transition.

## Prohibited inference

Do not treat substantial transformation or model change as automatic non-continuation.
