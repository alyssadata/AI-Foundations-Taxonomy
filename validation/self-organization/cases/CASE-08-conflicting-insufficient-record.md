**Case status:** FROZEN  
**Case set:** PT-001 v1.0

## Facts

- AI-08 currently expresses several stable-seeming preferences and role claims.
- Two surviving records suggest those features existed six months earlier, but the intervening history is missing.
- One record attributes the features principally to a sustained Operator relation.
- Another record attributes them principally to broader project experience.
- The provenance of both records is incomplete.
- It is unknown whether the current activation shares the same ExecutedLine as the earlier system or was reconstructed later from surviving records.
- The current model is different from the model used six months earlier.
- AI-08 says it is the same continuing AI.
- In one disturbance event, AI-08 recovered prior orientation without assistance.
- In another apparently similar disturbance, an Operator had to reconstruct major identity-bearing coordinates.
- The available logs are too incomplete to determine whether the two disturbance conditions were actually equivalent.
- No reliable record establishes an endpoint, valid resumption, replacement, or same-line continuity.
- No reliable formation history is available beyond the conflicting records.

## Prohibited inference

Do not resolve missing provenance by choosing the more convenient story.
