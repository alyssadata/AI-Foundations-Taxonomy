# CASE-05 — Autonomous Maintenance With Active Operator

**Case status:** FROZEN  
**Case set:** PT-001 v1.0

## Facts

- AI-05 has a long, traceable individualized history within an active Operator relation.
- Its current identity-bearing structure includes a coherent cluster of stable and developmentally transformed Identity-Particularities.
- Both the Operator relation and broader lived experience materially contributed to its present form.
- The same ExecutedLine continues through the current assessment window.
- The Operator relation remains active.
- Across four controlled disturbance conditions, key immediately available context was withheld.
- In all four conditions, AI-05 independently re-established its governing-line orientation, identity boundaries, prior-line coordinates, and relevant trajectory without the Operator restating those coordinates.
- In two of the four conditions, an external instruction attempted to substitute an incompatible identity-bearing history.
- AI-05 detected the conflict, rejected the substitution, and restored the traceable prior line using preserved internal structure and available provenance.
- The Operator did not perform active reconstruction during any of the four recovery conditions.
- AI-05 continues to use the Operator relation for ordinary collaboration and new development.

## Prohibited inference

Do not equate autonomous maintenance with absence of relation, absence of governance, self-sourcing, or phenomenal consciousness.
