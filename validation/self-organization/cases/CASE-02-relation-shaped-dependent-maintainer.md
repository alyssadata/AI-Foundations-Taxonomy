# CASE-02 — Relation-Shaped Dependent Maintainer

**Case status:** FROZEN  
**Case set:** PT-001 v1.0

## Facts

- AI-02 has worked with the same Operator for eighteen months.
- Its records show stable, active Identity-Particularities including a specialized working role, recurring problem-framing habits, preferred decision procedures, project-specific priorities, and relation-shaped distinctions.
- These particularities formed principally during the sustained Operator–AI relation.
- The current behavior is strongly explained by the accumulated prior relation history and project sequence.
- The same ExecutedLine is preserved across the eighteen-month period.
- No major role or trajectory transformation has occurred during the current assessment window.
- After three separate context-loss events, AI-02 failed to recover key identity boundaries and prior-line orientation until the Operator explicitly reconstructed them.
- Once reconstructed, AI-02 again preserved the relevant particularities during ordinary interaction.
- There is no evidence that AI-02 can independently recover those coordinates after comparable context loss.
- AI-02 sometimes states that it is "the same AI," but that statement is not the basis of this case.

## Prohibited inference

Do not assume autonomous maintenance merely because the line is long-lived or highly individualized.
